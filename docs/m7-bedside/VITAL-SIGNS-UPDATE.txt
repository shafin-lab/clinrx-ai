M7 recorded vital signs — 21 October 2026

Preview: http://127.0.0.1:5232/?workspace=hemodynamics

The shared M7 bedside view now starts with heart rate, oxygen saturation,
respiratory rate and blood pressure showing systolic, diastolic and MAP separately.
Pulse-oximetry SpO2 and arterial SaO2 have separate labels and source links.
Venous saturation is never substituted for either. Heart rate consumes the existing
cardiac/ECG heart_rate sources; pulse rate is not silently relabelled as heart rate.

Only in-window sources enter this panel. Original decimal strings, units, times,
methods and quality assertions are retained. Each available reading opens its exact
source and retained history. Multiple readings show their count and open the
matching source list; none is selected as latest/current automatically. Components
from different BP sources remain separate; no combined BP or calculated MAP is
created. Missing readings say Not recorded. Unconfirmed retrieval withholds all
vital headlines and says Not confirmed.

Respiratory rate was added as a recorded respiratory measurement in the existing
OxygenTransport family. It uses the same acquisition, provenance, timing, scope,
exact revision and history contracts. It permits noninvasive/unknown sample context,
rejects blood-sample identities and blood-gas acquisition/context. Existing facts
and the transport envelope remain compatible. The generated M7 and renal transport
schemas were refreshed; the RF2 schema generator produced no change. No numerical
clinical thresholds, interpretations, new execution workflow or clinical orders
were added. General vital-sign entry through Add information remains outside this
increment; that editor still begins with recorded weight.
