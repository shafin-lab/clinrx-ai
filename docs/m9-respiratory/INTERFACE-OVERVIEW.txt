ClinRx respiratory & ABG — approved bedside interface
23 October 2026 | Design v0.2 | Working M9, original M10

AUDIENCE
One common clinical view for intensivists, pulmonologists, physicians and
surgeons. Same facts and safeguards for everyone; expand advanced detail when
needed. The approved bedside overview is integrated into the existing synthetic workspace. Engineering verification and clinical validation are separate gates.

AT THE TOP
Patient and encounter | Recorded review window | Evidence confirmation

FOUR COMPACT PANELS

BREATHING SUPPORT
What was actually observed, and when?
Device / mode | oxygen concentration or uncertainty | flow | PEEP
Expand: set and measured values, support changes, interface and airway checks.

BLOOD GAS
Which specimen, and what support was present when it was collected?
Selected specimen/time | pH | specimen-specific CO2/O2 | bicarbonate/base excess
Sample-time oxygen and mode remain beside the gas. Later settings stay separate.
Expand: full panel, co-oximetry, lactate/Hb links, quality, method and source history.

LUNG MECHANICS
Can these measurements be used?
Available source assessment or explicit connection gap | measurement conditions
Expand: tidal volume/PBW, plateau, total PEEP, effort/leak/hold limitations.
Existing synthetic arithmetic must retain method/input/eligibility limits.
Clinical interpretation and treatment activation require separate validation.

REVIEW PLAN
What did the clinician assess, and what happens next?
Reviewer-entered concern | action | responsible reviewer | next review plan
Expand: targets, working cause, condition pathway, positioning and liberation.
An empty assessment remains “Not assessed”. No generated treatment instruction.

BELOW THE PANELS
Compact gas and actual support-change timeline; exact sample selection.
Evidence drawer for original results, context and revision history.
One short review form; advanced information opens only when needed.

FOR SURGICAL PATIENTS
Separate postoperative respiratory failure, prevention after planned extubation,
and deterioration after extubation. Link original surgery, airway, analgesia,
sedation, circulation and fluid records. Do not copy their facts into a new chart.

EVIDENCE AND CLARITY
Clear labels with familiar abbreviations; definitions on demand.
Key uncertainty, specimen type and sample oxygen always visible.
“Why?” shows the source/year and scope. Conditional guidance stays conditional.
Accurate provenance is not society accreditation or product clinical validation.

IMPLEMENTED AMENDMENT TO THE EXISTING WORKSPACE
Concurrent work has added respiratory source entry/review, physiology and
specialist tabs. Preserve that implementation; do not create a second workspace.
The compact overview now links to the existing sections. Reconcile source/eligibility behaviour
against the evidence crosswalk and acceptance inventory before clinical release.
Fluid balance/output stays in M8. M11–M18 identifiers remain unchanged.

CURRENT BOUNDARY
The four panels summarize existing evidence and clinician-authored reviews.
They add no new observation store or treatment activation. The complete shared
clinical workflow remains a separate acceptance gate; M7-C remains gated and M9
remains in progress. See IMPLEMENTATION-VERIFICATION.json for executed evidence.

OPTIONAL EXAMINATION AND IMAGING CAPTURE
The existing breathing view offers chest examination capture, including separate
right/left air entry and examination site/limitations. The investigation view
offers optional ultrasound, X-ray, CT/CTPA and MRI report fields. Blank fields
remain absent; an unperformed study can retain its explicit source reason. No
normal finding, completed test or image interpretation is generated. Each entry
uses the existing source identity, review-before-save and correction-history flow.
