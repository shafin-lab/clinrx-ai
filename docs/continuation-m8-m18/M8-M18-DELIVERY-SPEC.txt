ClinRx M8–M18 — connected delivery specification
21 October 2026 | Current user-supplied roadmap numbering

USE THE EXISTING PATIENT SHELL
M1–M5 are foundations; M6–M18 are capabilities supplying one patient picture.
Retain the current overview, grouped findings, exact source/history details and
clear unavailable/unconfirmed states. Add information will be one entry shell
with appropriately owned typed records. Imaging, medication safety, liver/graft
care and integrated reasoning remain cross-domain requirements.

The broader existing clinical requirements report is referenced in START-HERE.txt.
This document converts that direction into connected increments. It is not a
complete clinical treatment protocol, a statement of completion or a live-use release.
Future rule activation requires source-specific prerequisites and validation.

M8 — RENAL PHYSIOLOGY / CRRT
Reuse: bounded creatinine observations, recorded weight, urine and CRRT-context
events in the existing renal/fluid source catalog. Preserve raw values and times.
Extend: baseline creatinine with explicit selection/provenance, kidney history,
urine investigations, source-reported eGFR, current medications/nephrotoxic exposure,
obstruction/imaging and renal replacement modality, access, prescription, actual
running intervals, interruptions, solutions, anticoagulation and recorded machine data.
Connect CRRT fluid removal to M9, device/access to M12, nutrition to M14, glucose/
electrolytes to M17 and relevant drug levels to the same investigation record.
First acceptance: one CRRT source retains prescription, actual delivery and downtime
as distinct assertions; patient net removal never becomes gross effluent.
Withhold new AKI staging, clearance estimates and CRRT-dose calculations until their
baseline/weight/interval/unit/eligibility rules are separately established.

M9 — FLUID BALANCE / OUTPUT
Reuse: individual oral/enteral/IV/blood input, urine, drain, other loss and CRRT events.
Extend: episode/collection identity, interval precision, devices, source-reported
totals versus individual collections, omissions, correction history and completeness.
Link feeding water/flushes, administered products and relevant fluid-containing drugs
without treating a prescription as actual administration or counting one volume twice.
First acceptance: an individual drain collection and a source summary both remain
inspectable; neither is silently added to the other. Zero, not measured and unknown
are distinct. No balance calculation is enabled by this continuation.

M10 — RESPIRATORY / ABG
Reuse: existing circulatory oxygen/lactate and sample-time gas context when supplied.
Extend: airway identity, oxygen device/mode, source flow/FiO2, ventilation settings,
measured pressures/volumes, support changes, sample/specimen, collection time and
method, imaging/report links, secretion and airway/procedure context.
Link awakening/breathing assessments with M13 and airway/device history with M12.
First acceptance: an ABG opens its recorded sample-time settings; a later FiO2 is
not silently substituted. Arterial/venous/noninvasive readings stay distinguishable.
P/F ratios, predicted body weight, acid–base interpretation and ventilation advice
remain separately versioned capabilities requiring coherent inputs.

M11 — COAGULATION / TRANSFUSION
Reuse: source-reported product input where present, preserving actual supplied volume.
Extend: original coagulation/haemostasis assays, anticoagulant exposure, bleeding/
thrombotic assertions, TEG/ROTEM instrument/channel/reagent and time, blood-bank
reports, consent/reaction records and ordered/issued/administered product states.
Link procedures, organ support, liver/graft context and rare/prothrombotic results.
First acceptance: a transfusion's actual volume is linked once to fluid records;
units of product are not converted to an invented standard mL value. PT/INR, aPTT,
anti-Xa calibrators and viscoelastic parameters retain their assay identity.
Do not turn a thrombophilia finding into a diagnosis or an automatic anticoagulant
or transfusion recommendation. Clinical score/threshold policies need separate review.

M12 — DEVICES / DRAINS / PROCEDURES
Own one stable device or drain episode: type, site/laterality, indication, inserted/
changed/removed times with uncertainty, placement confirmation, status, settings,
complications, maintenance/check records and relevant procedure references.
Record procedure planned/performed/cancelled states, author, method, anatomy,
reports, complications and follow-up. Device identity is not a collection identity.
Include vascular/airway/feeding/urinary access, drains, CRRT/ECMO and neurological
devices as applicable; procedure/device types remain extensible.
Feeds use the recorded access episode; urine/drains use explicit source associations;
sedation uses the actual administration and monitoring context. No second device list
is created for nutrition or infection control.
First acceptance: replacement creates a new device episode while preserving the old
one; historical output retains its original association, and unresolved links remain.

M13 — SEDATION / ANALGESIA / DELIRIUM
Public benchmark sources inspected:
- Vanderbilt/CIBS publishes CAM-ICU, RASS resources, training and a VUMC pain/
  agitation/delirium order-set resource. Its public material supports a structured
  assessment workflow, not a claim about the current installed hospital EHR.
- UC San Diego's delirium education playbook is described on the Vanderbilt resource
  site. It supplies training/standardisation context; it is not a treatment engine.
- SCCM PADIS 2025 is an adult ICU focused update. PANDEM addresses critically ill
  infants and children separately. Adult instruments/policies are not universal.

Required owned records:
Pain instrument/version, score/components, location/context, assessor, time and
assessability; sedation target with reason and observed instrument/score; actual
analgesic/sedative/neuromuscular-blocker administrations with dose/route/formulation,
infusion changes and timing; delirium instrument/version, positive/negative/
unassessable/not performed result, components, reason and baseline/context.
Record awakening/breathing trial eligibility, deferred reason, actual occurrence and
response using approved local protocol; link mobility, sleep, sensory support,
family engagement, restraints, withdrawal surveillance and relevant adverse events.
Device measurements such as processed EEG, pupillometry or infusion-pump records
retain device/model, signal quality and timing. They do not replace bedside assessment.

Workflow: see recorded goals and current assessability, record assessment/action once,
review actual medication and support, then document response and review needs.
Keep neurological confounders visible in M16. Paralysis is not proof of comfort.
First acceptance: a positive screen followed by unassessability preserves both
states; no negative screen is invented. No screen automatically orders a medication.
Frequency, instrument rights/training, cutoffs and medication rules remain explicit
institutional activation items. No adult target is automatically applied to children.

M14 — NUTRITION
Recovery status:
- CAS-DOM-001 supplies the earlier Nutrition and Metabolism domain scope.
- NUT-STATE-001 supplies nutritional reserve/body composition/function/context.
- NUT-STATE-002 supplies metabolic state and explicitly excludes prescription and
  expenditure calculation within the state object.
- ADR-006 names Harris–Benedict as a calculation example, not a validated calculator.
- The existing 21 October clinical report proposes a wider equation library. This
  is additional planning, not recovery of the missing original formula inventory.
- sources/ is currently empty; the inspected local files do not establish where the
  full historic formula set is held. No formula was activated from memory.

Required connected records:
1. Assessment: intake/weight-change history, oral intake and assistance, allergies/
   restrictions, swallowing, gastrointestinal context, nutritional risk instrument,
   assessor/time and components. Keep risk screening separate from diagnosis.
2. Anthropometry: age/DOB provenance, measured/reported height and weight, usual/dry/
   selected calculation weight, method, times, oedema/ascites and uncertainty. A new
   weight does not silently replace the weight used for an earlier target.
3. Needs and targets: measured expenditure, estimated requirement and prescribed
   energy/protein/fluid targets are distinct records with validity periods, selected
   method/version, inputs, evidence, rationale and approving clinician/dietitian.
4. Calorimetry: device, measurement interval, VO2/VCO2 and reported expenditure,
   quality/calibration/limitations. A report is not automatically a valid estimate.
5. Route and formulation: oral/enteral/parenteral access, prescribed diet/product,
   formulation/version and supplied composition. Standard, concentrated, peptide or
   disease-labelled products form a local formulary, not automatic recommendations.
6. Delivery: prescription and actual delivery, route, bolus/infusion episodes, volume,
   product identity, intervals, interruptions/reasons/restart plan and missing coverage.
   Include recorded non-feed energy sources through original medication/support
   records, with no product-density or metabolic-contribution assumption.
7. Monitoring: tolerance symptoms, aspiration concerns, stool/vomiting/drain losses,
   glucose/triglyceride/electrolyte context, refeeding risk and recorded supplements,
   body composition/function, goals, response and next review.

Applicable contexts, selected from recorded patient data with explicit uncertainty:
Stable/noncritical adult; acute ICU/sepsis and resuscitation phase; postoperative/
perioperative; recovery/rehabilitation; obesity; kidney disease/CRRT; liver/transplant;
major trauma/burns/intestinal failure; pregnancy when applicable; infants/children.
No compulsory specialty mode. Missing age/context is a gap, not an adult default.
No one equation, protein target or escalation schedule serves every context.

Formula registry to establish, preserving the existing proposed inventory:
- Anthropometry, BMI and longitudinal weight change.
- Indirect calorimetry/selected Weir method and reported respiratory quotient.
- Versioned Mifflin–St Jeor, historical Harris–Benedict, selected ventilated-patient
  method and governed weight-based estimates where individually applicable.
- Recorded energy/protein targets versus eligible actual delivery, formulation
  composition, non-feed energy, glucose infusion rate and selected nitrogen methods.
- Separate age-appropriate paediatric methods; these are still to be curated.
For each: exact source/equation/version, supported population/phase, exclusions,
input units and provenance, valid time alignment, missingness rules, output precision,
worked cases and failure cases, reviewer and activation status. Calculated estimates
never become prescriptions by relabelling. No numeric engine is implemented here.

Apply the operational discipline of the sedation workflow: assess, record a goal,
reconcile delivered care, assess response and review. This is a ClinRx design proposal,
not a claim that a nutrition bundle has ABCDEF's evidence or validated outcomes.
First acceptance: a prescribed feed, delivered feed and an interruption appear through
one patient record; no calories are generated from a rate that merely was ordered.
Other cases: changed formulation; oedematous weight; new CRRT; refeeding monitoring;
feeds stopped while insulin continues; missing age; supplement from two source lists.

M15 — INFECTION CONTROL
Record precautions/isolation, reason/organism, active/ended periods, authoritative
policy version and responsible team; link screening/cultures and device episodes.
Capture recorded prevention actions/barriers and suspected versus confirmed events.
Do not equate colonisation with infection or apply surveillance definitions as bedside
diagnoses. Local infection-prevention definitions and cessation policies remain needed.
First acceptance: a corrected culture and changed precaution retain timing/reason;
an ended precaution is not silently carried into transfer as active.

M16 — NEUROLOGY
Record baseline, examination/components, pupils, motor/sensory findings, mental
status, assessor/time, instrument/version and assessability/confounders; seizure/EEG,
imaging reports, procedures, neurological device measurements and relevant medicines.
Preserve last-known-well, discovery, assessment and treatment times as distinct facts.
Connect sedative/paralytic exposure, gases, glucose/electrolytes and liver/renal context.
First acceptance: a sedated/unassessable component is not recorded as normal or as a
new proven neurological deficit. Pending/corrected imaging stays clearly identified.
Stroke windows, seizure-treatment logic and prognostication are separate protocols,
not inferred from displaying the available data together.

M17 — ENDOCRINE
Minimum connected scope:
- Diabetes type/history, actual insulin and other therapy, glucose source (lab/POC/
  CGM), value/unit/time, quality and confirmation; HbA1c with source context.
- Meals/enteral/PN/dextrose, glucocorticoids, catecholamines, kidney/liver function,
  interrupted delivery and recorded hypoglycaemic events/treatment/response.
- DKA/HHS-related recorded ketones, blood gases, sodium/potassium, osmolality,
  medications/exposure, volume state and neurological context; no automatic diagnosis.
- Adrenal/pituitary history, steroid exposure/formulation/timing/taper, cortisol/ACTH
  and stimulation/suppression protocol with actual doses and sample times.
- Thyroid studies and therapies, assay/illness/drug-interference context; pituitary,
  gonadal, bone/mineral and rare endocrine reports as clinically supplied.
- Water balance: serum/urine osmolality, sodium, urine output, relevant therapies,
  recorded volume assessment and interval uncertainty; link the same M8/M9 records.
- Pumps/CGM: device episode, source quality, capability/oversight record and backup
  plan where applicable; device measurements do not automatically authorise dosing.

Use separate applicability for adult noncritical/critical and paediatric/pregnancy
care. The Endocrine Society 2022 inpatient hyperglycaemia resource inspected here
specifically concerns noncritical adults; it is not a universal insulin protocol.
First acceptance: feed cessation and ongoing insulin are visible together from exact
sources. A future review prompt needs its own rule/evidence and monitored response
path. No insulin adjustment, crisis classification, calculated osmolality, sodium
correction advice or endocrine diagnosis is activated in this increment.

M18 — DISCHARGE / TRANSFER
Assemble course/problems, current supports, medications and intended changes,
devices/drains, precautions, nutrition/functional needs, pending results and their
follow-up owner, monitoring and destination requirements from the same record.
Retain time of preparation, source cutoff, author/reviewer and unresolved gaps.
Update after a material correction; do not silently alter a signed historical summary.
First acceptance: transfer reuses the record without a manual selection/transcription
step. A result returning later has an accountable follow-up route; generation alone
does not establish receipt, sign-off or responsibility transfer.

CONNECTED ENGINEERING SEQUENCE
A. Completed in this increment: reusable routine-to-rare product catalog and explicit
   presentation mappings consumed by the existing overview. Existing strict admission
   rules remain intact. Broad imports are not enabled by catalog coverage.
B. Subsequently authorised and implemented: recorded weight through the existing
   RF2 owner, including corrections and local recovery. See WEIGHT-ENTRY-IMPLEMENTED.txt
   for the exact synthetic gate, verification and remaining entry-type scope.
C. General shared investigations: implement the versioned envelope, original report
   owner, bounded assembly and exact history together. Integrate compatible M6 data
   only with explicit same-patient/encounter/repository/cutoff correspondence.
D. Add one owned source-to-overview slice at a time: device/access episodes, sedation
   assessment, nutrition target/delivery and endocrine measurement/therapy context.
   No independent module demos. Unsupported fields stay explicit gaps.
E. Calculations and clinical interpretation follow their separate eligibility,
   evidence/version, conflict and validation contracts. No execution here.
