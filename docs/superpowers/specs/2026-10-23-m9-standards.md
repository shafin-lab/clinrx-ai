# M9 respiratory standards-to-feature map

Evidence review: 23 October 2026. Companion to `2026-10-23-m9-respiratory-design.md`. This is a bounded source review and implementation specification, not clinical validation, a complete guideline library, or an assertion that every recommendation has been implemented. Primary guideline/technical-standard reports and society-hosted copies are linked below; PMC links host the original reports rather than third-party summaries. Older Indian society documents are explicitly dated.

## Organisations and claim boundary

- **ACCP/CHEST** = American College of Chest Physicians. **ERS** = European Respiratory Society. **ATS** = American Thoracic Society. **ICS in this project = Indian Chest Society**, as confirmed by the user; **NCCP(I)** = National College of Chest Physicians (India); **IAB** = Indian Association for Bronchology. The [official Indian Chest Society guideline index](https://www.indianchestsociety.com/guidelines.php) verifies the relevant joint documents.
- The UK Intensive Care Society in BTS/ICS guidance is a different organisation. Indian Society of Critical Care Medicine (**ISCCM**) is also different. Neither is a substitute attribution for Indian Chest Society. In asthma/COPD medication text, “ICS” can additionally mean inhaled corticosteroids; expand the term in labels.
- AARC, ESICM, SCCM and PALICC/PALISI documents can supplement the requested societies but must retain their actual authorship. The 2026 ERS NIV handbook is educational reference material, not a new clinical practice guideline.
- Safe product wording: “Source-linked respiratory evidence and transparent physiology calculations; references include the dated documents below.” Do not claim “CHEST/ERS/ICS approved”, “society endorsed”, “clinically validated”, regulatory clearance, or comprehensive guideline compliance. Unit tests establish software behaviour, not clinical performance.

## Calculation prerequisites and limitations

The source/time/quality boundary in the design is clinically material: measured, set, source-calculated and app-calculated values need separate display; pH and blood-gas bicarbonate may come from different analytical pathways from chemistry total CO2. Every derived result must expose exact input names, units, source revision and collection time, formula/version and withheld reason. A record title such as “ABG” is insufficient proof of arterial specimen type. Unknown population, duplicate concepts, censored results and suspect/withdrawn evidence must not become ordinary numeric inputs.

- **Mechanics:** calculated driving pressure/static compliance requires confirmed passive conditions and occlusion with measured total PEEP; resistance additionally needs a verified constant inspiratory flow in compatible units. These are deliberate conservative implementation gates. The original [2020 APS investigation](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/japplphysiol.00059.2020) describes zero-flow occlusions and driving pressure as Pplat minus total PEEP; [CHEST original occlusion-method validation (1992)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012369216391334), DOI 10.1378/chest.102.3.918, is methodological evidence, not an ACCP clinical guideline. Assisted-breath measurements require additional method-specific interpretation; they must not be silently processed as passive mechanics.
- **Acid-base:** expected compensation is a conditional model, not a diagnosis, chronology detector or assertion of a single disorder. The original Winter-related report is [Albert, Dell and Winters, 1967, Quantitative displacement of acid-base equilibrium in metabolic acidosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6016545/), DOI 10.7326/0003-4819-66-2-312. Its bibliographic identity was verified; the full original equation passage was not accessible in this review. The [original chronic hypercapnia study (1966)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJM196607212750301) confirms physiological study provenance, not a universal bedside compensation slope. Do not attribute Winter, anion-gap, oxygen-transport or other basic physiology equations to CHEST/ERS/ICS endorsement merely because those societies appear elsewhere in this map.
- **Oxygenation/advanced calculations:** PaO2/FiO2 needs arterial PO2 plus explicit sample-time FiO2, and does not itself diagnose or grade ARDS without the rest of the relevant definition. Alveolar gas calculations need explicit barometric pressure/RQ/water-vapour assumptions. Shunt/dead-space and transport calculations require the correct arterial/mixed-venous/expired-gas specimen and method. Specialist ECMO/ECCO2R, sleep scoring and paediatric/neonatal classifications remain reported evidence unless a separate current population-specific protocol is implemented and verified.

## Clinician-facing acceptance criteria

1. Start with respiratory support, clinical course and latest interpretable gas; make sample-time context visible beside each calculation. Keep physician/surgeon bedside review concise and specialist PFT/procedure/sleep detail accessible without hiding missing prerequisites.
2. Display original evidence, derived physiology and signed clinician assessment separately. Source/history opens the exact record version, and historical views use only evidence available by that cutoff.
3. Use “not calculated — arterial specimen not confirmed”, “sample-time FiO2 missing”, or an equally specific reason. Do not replace missing data with a normal/green result or a failed clinical screen.
4. Narrative capture is sufficient to preserve a specialist report; it is not proof that the app independently interprets its content. Do not label unsupported tests as automatically interpreted.
5. For any future rule that changes clinical recommendations, show source title/year, patient population and prerequisites; resolve newer versus older or differing society recommendations explicitly. Clinical expert review, intended-use assessment and prospective evaluation remain outstanding before any claim of clinical validation.

## Review limitations

This review identified the official society index and the primary reports above; it did not establish that no newer local or disease-specific guidance exists. Some ERS full-text fetches returned access errors although publisher-indexed text and exact identifiers were available. The 2023 lung-volume correction was checked: it corrects spirometry acceptability/usability criteria used for RV/TLC in table 3; any future laboratory algorithm must use the corrected table. Complete acquisition standards, all rare respiratory diseases, paediatric/neonatal physiology, ECMO management, thoracic oncology staging and drug/dose/order recommendations are outside this mapping. No treatment order, medication dose or automatic escalation decision is specified here.
