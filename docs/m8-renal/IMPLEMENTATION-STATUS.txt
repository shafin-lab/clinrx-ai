WHAT IS IMPLEMENTED
The actual shared candidate has a Kidney review beside Patient overview and
Hemodynamic review. The URL is ?workspace=renal. It uses the same current shared
RF2 catalog in authoring mode and the existing RF1 combined service when enabled
in standalone mode. It does not fetch a second catalog when switching views.

Four cards cover kidney status, urine/collection, kidney support and the next
review. Original creatinine, blood-gas kidney context, interval urine records,
CRRT-context/net-removal, weights and authored renal notes remain linked to their
original owners. Cumulative urine is not summed with intervals. No weight is
automatically selected for calculations. Original values, offsets and revisions
are retained. Missing volume is not zero. Evidence groups expose contextual and
unplaced sources without assigning them to invented hourly buckets.

The drawer opens exact source/history through the RF2 owner or original RF1
history route. It cancels late results when selection/context changes. Failed
retrieval, scope/cutoff/gate mismatch, duplicate identities or conflicting catalog
content blocks the affected bedside projection/review rather than mixing patients
or substituting a different version. A changed snapshot retains draft text but
blocks submission until explicit new review. Existing source-entry recovery and
pending-command guards remain in the shared patient workspace.

Manual renal review records require next step, reviewer, rationale and next review
plan. They pin the original renal evidence and immutable note text. They survive
in-page navigation between overview, M7 and M8, but CLEAR ON PAGE RELOAD. They are
not durable chart entries, scheduled tasks, clinical sign-off or team notifications.

DESIGN CATALOGS, NOT ACTIVATED CAPABILITIES

Typed history/baseline selection, complete specimen/patency metadata, governed

CRRT/citrate/calcium/device segments, delivered-dose calculations and durable
clinical reviews/tasks remain staged extensions. Existing context entries do not
provide those records. The official KDIGO status page inspected on this date still
describes the 2026 update as preparing for publication; its public-review draft is
excluded from the proposed published/local-approval profile authority.

No new backend schemas/routes, clinical source writes, orders, fluid-removal
adjustments, dialysis initiation or device commands were added by M8. Clinical
execution stays disabled. The offline arithmetic examples are engineering checks
only and are not imported by ClinRx. The design is not permission for patient use.
