BEDside behaviour
The urine card opens a full-width daily chart. Hours run horizontally (08–09,
09–10, etc.), with date, start hour and explicit fixed-offset chart clock.
Intake, output, net and running recorded net are shown in mL. Positive, negative
and zero differences are labelled. The net is included intake minus included
output; it is a recorded subtotal, never a claim of complete patient balance.
Missing cells remain blank/dash, and a documented zero remains zero. Source
minutes under urine show interval coverage, not certified collection completeness.
The chart scrolls internally on a small screen, retaining its row labels.
Selecting an hourly number expands contributors and their original source/history.
Original urine collections also use compact rows in the source drawer.

SOURCE AND CALCULATION CONTRACT
The existing shared M8 assessment stays on its original window. The daily chart
performs a separate read of an explicit 24-hour RF2 source window from the same
repository and patient/encounter. It does not call a two-hour view a full day.
Scope/cutoff mismatches and changed or unavailable shared evidence block use.
Late responses cannot replace the currently selected day. In-page navigation
reuses a settled daily read; an explicit refresh, changed day or shared cutoff
retrieves it again. Daily sources are not silently added to the existing M8
assessment note's evidence pins.

Only source-recorded typed volume categories are admitted. mL and L are converted
by explicit mapping using bounded decimal rational arithmetic. Original quantity,
unit, revision and source are retained; unresolved mappings remain labelled.
Wrong dimensions, missing quantities, negative values, conflicting units or
unresolved timing are excluded. Missing is never inferred as zero.
Start is inclusive and end exclusive for point entries. Source intervals inside
one hour are assigned to that hour. Collections across hours enter the daily
total once in Across hours, without invented hourly prorating. Collections
crossing the day boundary are excluded rather than apportioned. Possible
overlapping/duplicate collections in the same category are unresolved and
excluded until source independence can be established. Cumulative source urine
and balance summaries are separate and never added to individual entries.
CRRT net patient removal is an output category; gross filter effluent is separate.
Running hourly net omits unallocated intervals and is not carried into empty hours.
Full-day completeness remains Not established with the current legacy contracts.
Insensible losses, irrigation adjustment, medication-carrier reconciliation and
admission-to-date balance are not inferred. M9 should reuse this source-owned
projection and its rules rather than introduce another calculation of the same
entries. Local calendar/DST schedules need a governed timezone extension; the
current selector explicitly represents 24 elapsed hours at a fixed offset.

CREATININE DISPLAY
Creatinine cards use mg/dL with two decimal places. Explicit supported raw units
can be converted using 88.4 micromol/L per mg/dL; 150 micromol/L displays approximately
1.70 mg/dL. Raw values are retained in source details. Unknown or conflicting
units are withheld, never guessed. Display conversion of an unresolved raw unit
label retains its unresolved status and is not a validated clinical input.
This display profile does not activate AKI classification, clearance or dosing.
Urea and BUN remain distinct analytes and must not share numeric conversions by
simple relabelling.
