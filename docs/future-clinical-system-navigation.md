# Clinician-facing system navigation

The completed M7–M10 areas use these top-level labels in the connected patient
workspace and patient overview:

- **Cardiovascular system** — M7 hemodynamics and bedside review.
- **Renal system** — M8 kidney physiology, CRRT, fluid balance and output.
- **Respiratory system** — M9 respiratory support, blood gases and related review.
- **Hematologic system** — M10 blood products, coagulation and transfusion.
- **Antimicrobials** — culture review, prescriptions and administrations, and the
  antimicrobial reference library.

The word “hemodynamics” remains available for the clinical workflow within the
cardiovascular system. Culture review and antimicrobial treatment workflows are
grouped under the requested top-level **Antimicrobials** label.

This is a presentation-only change. Preserve workspace query routes, module IDs,
clinical record kinds, saved-review domains and persisted identifiers so existing
links, records and reviews remain readable. Verify the connected patient journey
and saved-review behavior after label changes.
